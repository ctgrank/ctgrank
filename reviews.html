What you think of Shikhor

Shikhor is built and maintained by one person. If a rank looks wrong, a year is missing, or something helped you find your result — say so here. Replies are open, so questions get answered.

Before you post

  • Correcting a result? Include the exam, year, roll number and what looks wrong. Vague reports can't be checked.
  • Shikhor is unofficial. It republishes board data — it can't change your marks. For that, contact the Chattogram board directly.
  • No personal data in public posts. Don't paste phone numbers, addresses or full marksheets.

Posting uses a GitHub account — it keeps the site free of spam and bots. Don't have one? They're free and take a minute to create.
