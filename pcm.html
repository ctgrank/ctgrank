How each category is ordered

A rank is 1 + the number of eligible students ahead of you. Ties break on the subjects listed, in that order — so two students on the same PCM total separate on Higher Math first.

  • PCM ALL — grades first, then PCM marks. The overall science standing.
  • Only PCM Mark — PCM total alone, GPA ignored. What most people mean by "PCM rank".
  • BUET — needs GPA 5.00 and A+ in all three. BUET BME adds A in Biology.
  • RUET — A in each of P, C, M and 13.00 grade points between them.
  • KUET — ranks on PCME, so English marks count. 16.00 grade points.
  • CUET — 14.00 PCM grade points and at least B in English.
  • BUTEX — GPA 4.00, A- in all four PCME subjects, 16.50 grade points.
  • Each BME variant adds A in Biology to its parent category.

About the data

Ranks are calculated from published board results — the same marksheet data behind every list on this site. Grades are derived from those marks using the board grading scale, because the result sheets carry the overall GPA rather than a per-subject grade.

The all-board combined rank needs the marks of every board. Only the boards listed as loaded above are included; the rest show as unavailable until their data is added, so a combined rank is never guessed.
