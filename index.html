About Shikhor

Built & copyrighted by Jahin.

Shikhor (Bengali for summit) is an independent, unofficial study tool that re-publishes publicly available Chattogram (Chittagong) Education Board results as searchable merit lists — for SSC/Dakhil and HSC/Alim, across Science, Business Studies (Commerce) and Humanities (Arts).

Search instantly by student name, roll number or institution, open any student for their subject-wise marks, compare two results, explore the top-performing institutions, and export anything you see to CSV. Every table is mobile-friendly, every result has a shareable link.

How the rank is calculated

Students are ordered by total marks (descending); ties are broken by GPA (descending) and then by roll number (ascending). Students with the same total marks and the same GPA share one rank.

Data coverage & honesty

Some source years are incomplete — a dataset is tagged Partial when records are missing from the published list, and the exact coverage percentage is shown next to it. Where the source has no subject-wise marks, the student card simply omits that section instead of guessing.
